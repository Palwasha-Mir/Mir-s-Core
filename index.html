About Mir's Core

Based in Sialkot with global export capabilities, **Mir's Core** stands at the pinnacle of luxury activewear manufacturing. We combine cutting-edge technical fabric engineering with master craftsmanship to produce high-end yoga wear, tracksuits, compression sets, and tennis apparel for discerning international brands.

Our state-of-the-art production facility ensures strict quality control, sustainable dyeing processes, and precision stitching that meets the exacting standards of top-tier global retailers.
